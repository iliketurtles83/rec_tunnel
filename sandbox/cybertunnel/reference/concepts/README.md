## Prompts

All prompts share the framing: *Third-person chase camera view behind a tiny dark arrowhead
spaceship with two thin white engine trails, centred low in the frame, …* and end with
*Cyberspace, 3D render, emissive neon line art, strong bloom, … cinematic, no text.*

- **Crystal field:** flying low through a vast dark void filled with floating orange neon
  wireframe polyhedra: icosahedra, octahedra and triangular prisms of many sizes, drawn as thin
  glowing orange and amber edge lines with faint translucent faces. Clusters of shapes line both
  sides, leaving a clear open flight lane straight ahead toward a soft distant warm glow at the
  vanishing point. Sparse white speed streaks. Deep black-blue background.
- **Circuit canyon:** a giant abstract circuit-board canyon: walls on left and right are towering
  dark slabs etched with glowing white and pale cyan circuit traces, square solder pads and chip
  outlines; the floor is a dark board with neon traces converging toward the vanishing point;
  above, bridges of glowing ribbon cables cross the canyon. A narrow open flight lane down the
  middle. Black background with dark teal tones.
- **Signal sea:** over a dark ocean of data: a black reflective surface below with long rolling
  wave lines drawn in thin glowing violet and cyan neon, like an oscilloscope landscape of sine
  waves receding to the horizon. Above, a black sky with faint horizontal scanlines and giant thin
  neon arcs spanning overhead like bridges at intervals. Wide open space, clear flight lane ahead.
- **Hex conduit:** a colossal hexagonal data conduit: the tunnel walls are made of thin glowing
  cyan neon hexagon outlines receding in perspective, with occasional hexagon cells filled with
  faint scrolling data panels, magenta circuit traces running lengthwise along the walls, and
  bright white seams where wall segments meet. Center of the tunnel is clear and dark, leading
  toward a pale cyan glow at the vanishing point.
